WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM THE FIRST ATTEMPT
----------------------------------------------
The recorder clicks each transition AT the mark time, so every UI
animation plays in the first ~2 seconds of its slot, then the screen
is FROZEN (static) until the next mark. The first cue sheet started
each narration line AT the mark and let it run 10-20s long, so every
payoff line landed long after the picture had already resolved.

Rule used here: for each beat, the KEY PHRASE lands within about 1
second of the transition mark. That means most lines START 2-10
seconds BEFORE their mark (spoken over the outgoing/frozen frame),
with the payoff word landing on the cut. After the cut, remaining
exposition is kept short and is delivered over the now-frozen new
frame; deliberate silence is left rather than racing to fill time.

Measured transition marks used for this timing (seconds):
  0   rest-state
  12  heartbeats
  32  payment -> TREASURY
  56  window-lapses / SEALED
  80  THE DEFLECTION (peak; completes ~82)
  112 liquidation
  132 waterfall pours
  156 resolved
  172 /vitals (live agent)
  186 /register
  213.68 end of video

HOW TO USE THIS SHEET
----------------------
- IN / OUT are absolute seconds into the (unchanged) video.
- "MARK" names which transition the KEY PHRASE (marked **like this**)
  is timed to land on/near.
- Read at a measured, unhurried pace — slower than the AI reference
  track if anything. The AI guide track
  (media/GUIDE-v1-aivoice-DO-NOT-SUBMIT.mp4) is a TIMING REFERENCE
  ONLY, not for submission (ETHGlobal bans AI voiceover) — record your
  own voice against it, matching where the bracketed key phrases land.
- Gaps between OUT and the next IN are intentional silence over a
  frozen frame. Don't fill them — let the picture sit.
