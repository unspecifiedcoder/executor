WHY THE LINES START BEFORE THEIR MARKS
----------------------------------------------
The recorder fires each transition AT its mark, so each animation
plays in the first ~2s of a slot and the screen is then comparatively
static. A segment therefore STARTS 2-4 seconds BEFORE its mark, so the
setup words play over the outgoing frame and the payoff word lands on
the cut. Starting a line at its mark puts the payoff 10-20s late — the
defect this method avoids (see v1's first attempt).

Method: generate each segment's TTS, use `edge-tts --write-subtitles`
to get per-clause timestamps, compute
  start = mark - offset_of_key_phrase_within_the_clip
then iterate (trim words, regenerate) wherever a segment's tail
collided with the next segment's setup.

ONE DELIBERATE DEVIATION (flagged, not hidden)
----------------------------------------------
Marks 140 and 158 are both nominally "the same dwell" in the source
brief (140 = receipts still dwelling, no new transition; 158 = the
real cut to ETHERSCAN). The 3.0s silence rule after "Estate" (mark
120) is a hard requirement and takes priority: honoring it forces
Segment 9 to start at 123.95s, which lands its own key phrase
("thirty-seven blocks apart") around 132s — about 8s ahead of the
140 dwell-mark rather than on top of it. Since 140 is a continuation

a real transition to a new page and lands exactly on-mark), this
was judged the correct trade-off: the two-receipts numbers are still
spoken well inside the 120-158 dwell window, and the harder
requirement (silence over the peak) is met exactly. Mark 158
(ETHERSCAN) lands its key word ("Etherscan") within 0s of the cut.

HOW TO USE THIS SHEET
----------------------
- IN / OUT are absolute seconds into the (unchanged) video.
- "MARK" names which transition the KEY PHRASE (marked **like this**)
  is timed to land on/near.
- Read at a measured, unhurried pace, "brainrot" cadence: short
  declaratives, one idea each, flat delivery — the pauses carry the
  meaning, not vocal emphasis. The AI guide track
  (media/GUIDE-v3-aivoice-DO-NOT-SUBMIT.mp4) is a TIMING REFERENCE
  ONLY, not for submission (ETHGlobal bans AI voiceover) — record your
  own voice against it, matching where the bracketed key phrases land.
- Gaps between OUT and the next IN are intentional silence over a
  static or dwelling frame. Don't fill them — let the picture sit.
