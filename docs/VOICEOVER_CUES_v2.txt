WHY THE LINES START BEFORE THEIR MARKS
----------------------------------------------
The recorder clicks each transition AT the mark time, so every UI
animation plays in the first ~2 seconds of its slot, then the screen
is FROZEN (static) until the next mark. If narration started AT the
mark, the payoff word would land 10-20s late, well after the picture
had already resolved (this was the bug in an earlier attempt).

Rule used here: for each beat, the KEY PHRASE lands within about 1
second of the transition mark. In v2 the marks are much closer
together than in the v1 cut (gaps of 10-24s instead of 12-32s), so
almost every segment leads with its key phrase as the very first
word or two of the clip (e.g. "Sealed.", "Liquidation.", "Runs.",
"Resolves.") with only a fraction of a second of lead-in, then the
rest of the sentence trails AFTER the cut, spoken over the next
frozen frame. This still satisfies "lands within ~1s of the mark" —
it just means almost no setup plays before the cut in the tightest
windows. Where a window is wide (the deflection, 56→80s), there's
room for the fuller sentence before the payoff and a long, unhurried
tail after it.

Measured transition marks used for this timing (seconds), from the
recorder's own log:
  0     overview at rest (destination = treasury)
  10    step 03 - eighteen heartbeats
  26    step 04 - payment lands in TREASURY
  42    step 06 - window lapses, treasury SEALED
  56    step 07 - THE DEFLECTION (peak)
  80    step 08 - liquidation + heartbeat flatline
  94    step 09 - executePlan waterfall pours
  112   step 10 - resolved
  130   /vitals - the live agent, still beating
  142   /register - form starts being typed live
  158   form fully filled; dwell on completed plan
  190.72 end

HOW TO USE THIS SHEET
----------------------
- IN / OUT are absolute seconds into the (unchanged) video.
- "MARK" names which transition the KEY PHRASE (marked **like this**)
  is timed to land on/near.
- Read at a measured, unhurried pace. The AI guide track
  (media/GUIDE-v2-aivoice-DO-NOT-SUBMIT.mp4) is a TIMING REFERENCE
  ONLY, not for submission (ETHGlobal bans AI voiceover) — record
  your own voice against it, matching where the bracketed key
  phrases land.
- Gaps between OUT and the next IN are intentional silence over a
  frozen frame. Don't fill them — let the picture sit.
